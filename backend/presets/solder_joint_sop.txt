WI-EL-031  LED Driver Harness: Lead Termination Soldering (Bench 2)

Purpose: Terminate the red supply lead onto the driver terminal held in the helping-hands
metal clamp. Cold joints from rushed heating are the top cause of intermittent field failures.

Equipment: temperature-controlled soldering iron (350 C), flux tin, helping-hands metal clamp.

1. Clean and wet the soldering iron tip by dipping it into the flux tin for at least 1 second.
2. Hold the iron tip on the joint in the metal clamp for at least 2.0 seconds so the joint reaches
   wetting temperature before feeding solder. Do not lift early.
3. Let the joint cool undisturbed and inspect for a shiny concave fillet.

SOP-ME-214  Electronics Enclosure: Mounting Plate Fastening (Station 4)

Purpose: Secure the left component mounting plate to the enclosure base before the PCB is installed.
Loose plates cause board flex and vibration failures in field returns.

Tools: Phillips #1 screwdriver (orange handle). Parts: 4x M3 x 6 pan-head screws.

1. Place one M3 screw into each of the four corner holes of the left mounting plate.
2. Using the screwdriver, drive all four corner screws until seated. Keep the driver engaged on each
   screw for at least 1.5 seconds so the screw reaches seating torque.
3. Visually confirm no corner hole is left empty before passing the enclosure to Station 5.
